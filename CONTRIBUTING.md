# Contributing

Sources & Uses is currently founder-maintained. Small corrections to public educational content or the garden implementation may be proposed through a pull request.

Before proposing a change:

- do not submit confidential information, employer materials, real dataroom data, copied interview tests, candidate work, or paid course material;
- identify the source and licence for factual or third-party material;
- keep commercial cases and solutions out of this public repository;
- run `npm run verify`;
- explain the user-facing purpose and provide screenshots for visual changes.

By submitting original work, a contributor confirms they have the right to provide it for inclusion under the licensing terms identified for that file. No contribution should imply endorsement by an employer or third party.
