# Deliverable 8 — Validation plan

## Objective

Test whether candidates will pay for an audited, realistic, integrated case—not whether they like PE content. The experiment must distinguish interest in cases, Excel tooling and coaching/feedback.

## Scope, cap and timing

- duration: 6–8 weeks;
- founder-time cap: 120 hours;
- external cash cap: CHF 3,000;
- product: one case only;
- no production account platform, add-in or AI;
- no paid advertising before an organic purchase conversion is observed.

## Assets

1. Brand-led landing page with three testable value propositions.
2. 15-minute free diagnostic and concise debrief.
3. Paid full-case outline and 8–12 polished sample pages.
4. Complete paid case before accepting non-refundable fulfilment; preorders must state delivery date and refund rights clearly.
5. 90-second screen-recorded viewer demo.
6. Clickable add-in prototype showing five P0 commands; no functioning promise.
7. Two price/offer variants.
8. Partner kit: one-page description, screenshots, disclosure language and tracked links.

## Hypotheses

The original suggestion of 100 free downloads and 10–15 preorders is directionally useful but incomplete. Downloads without qualified traffic and completion can be gamed. The revised primary gate uses 150 qualified visitors, eight actual paid orders, product usage, and partner-sourced traffic. The stretch gate remains 100 completed free diagnostics and 15 paid orders.

## Experiment design

### 1. Interviews before copy finalisation

- 15–20 people who completed or attempted a European PE case within 24 months;
- at least five bankers, five consultants/adjacent professionals and five students;
- maximum five warm contacts to reduce politeness bias;
- show current alternatives and ask participants to spend/choose, not rate ideas;
- record current spend, actual preparation artefacts, time constraints and whether add-ins were permitted.

### 2. Message test

Randomly split qualified visitors:

- A: “European mid-market PE case library”;
- B: “Rehearse the full investment decision under time pressure.”

Keep screenshots, price and call-to-action constant. Primary metric is paid checkout start/order; email capture is secondary. Do not declare a winner from fewer than roughly 100 visitors per arm unless the effect is overwhelming; use the result directionally at small sample size.

### 3. Price/offer test

Use sequential or randomised cohorts with clear delivery equivalence:

- full case CHF 59 beta;
- full case CHF 79;
- mock premium CHF 129 including future add-in access versus CHF 129 including second advanced case.

Never charge for a non-functioning add-in. Premium selection can be a refundable deposit/waitlist choice until production is authorised.

### 4. Free diagnostic funnel

```text
qualified landing visitor
 -> starts diagnostic
 -> completes diagnostic
 -> requests debrief / consents to marketing separately
 -> views paid preview
 -> starts checkout
 -> pays
 -> starts full case
 -> unlocks solution/debrief
```

Capture referrer/partner code and funnel step. Avoid session replay.

### 5. Partner outreach

University/club list: HSG, Bocconi, WHU, LSE, LBS, Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, HEC, INSEAD, IE, ESADE, Mannheim, Frankfurt, RSM and CBS, plus 10 smaller clubs where response competition is lower.

Offer:

- free diagnostic for members;
- unique 15% member discount;
- 20% commission or equivalent club sponsorship credit;
- no required live workshop;
- optional 30-minute recorded debrief;
- no use of university logo without permission.

Track time spent per active partner. Kill the channel if it requires more than two founder hours per paid order after the initial setup.

### 6. Recruiter test

Interview 8–10 European finance recruiters. Ask them to critique the free diagnostic and optionally share it. Do not ask for endorsement first. A positive statement without tracked sharing or procurement interest is not validation.

## Measurement definitions

- **Qualified visitor:** matches target geography/role or arrives through a relevant club/creator/search term and views at least the product section; exclude bots and founder/test traffic.
- **Lead:** verified email plus diagnostic completion; newsletter consent tracked separately.
- **Strong purchase intent:** completed checkout or refundable deposit, not survey “likely to buy.”
- **Partner:** sends tracked traffic or commits a dated distribution action; a friendly reply is not a partner.
- **Completion:** solution unlocked after minimum meaningful case engagement, supplemented by self-report; timer data is not proctoring proof.
- **Defect:** contradiction, wrong number, broken file/formula, ambiguous instruction or material cross-asset mismatch.

## Decision gates

### Gate A — problem/positioning

Proceed to finish the paid case only if 12+ interviews reveal repeated full-case rehearsal pain and at least half used/paid for structured preparation.

### Gate B — willingness to pay

Proceed to deliver beta only if at least eight orders/deposits are achieved from 150 qualified visitors or 15 orders overall without paid acquisition.

### Gate C — product quality

Proceed to Phase 2 only if:

- at least 10 buyers start;
- at least 40% of starters complete/debrief;
- median realism and usefulness each >=8/10;
- no unresolved critical numerical defect;
- refund/serious-defect rate <=8%;
- at least two partners send real traffic;
- at least 60% can name a distinctive paid outcome.

### Gate D — platform

Build accounts/viewer only after Gate C and at least 25 cumulative paid orders or a credible university cohort contract.

### Gate E — add-in

Apply the separate add-in gate in Deliverable 6. Survey excitement is insufficient.

## Failure actions

- Good diagnostic demand, weak paid conversion: improve/replace paid outcome once; do not build platform.
- Paid interest, low completion: shorten/restructure the case; test once.
- Strong demand for human review: do not turn into coaching by default. Test a constrained reviewer marketplace/office-hour price only if it remains operationally light.
- Add-in demand but weak case demand: do not pivot immediately; compare against QuickCel/Arixcel with a separate paid landing test.
- No partner traffic and no organic conversion: stop.

## Tooling for validation

Buy rather than build:

- landing page: Framer/Webflow/Carrd or a minimal clean static app;
- checkout/tax: merchant of record;
- email: simple consent-aware provider;
- scheduling/interviews: existing scheduler;
- prototype: Figma;
- analytics: privacy-minimal first-party funnel events;
- files: protected links/manual fulfilment for the first beta.

The validation stack should be disposable. It is evidence collection, not the MVP architecture.
