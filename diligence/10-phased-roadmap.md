## Phase 2 — Minimal commercial product

Entry condition: Gate C plus 25 cumulative paid buyers or one credible cohort contract.

Duration estimate: 6–10 weeks part-time after content is stable.

Build:

- clean private application repository;
- passwordless account and minimal dashboard;
- MoR webhooks and entitlements;
- one or two cases;
- authenticated page viewer with watermark;
- controlled downloads and solution unlock;
- minimal first-party analytics, issue reporting and admin actions;
- project-specific tests, runbook, privacy/terms/refund documentation.

Do not build add-in, AI, community, general CMS, subscriptions or advanced affiliate system.

Gate:

- 75+ cumulative buyers;
- rolling three-month gross revenue >=CHF 2,000/month or a contracted university cohort;
- contribution margin >=80% before founder time;
- refunds <=6%;
- at least 40% starter-to-debrief completion;
- support <=20 minutes per order;
- repeatable second case delivered with <=80% of first-case authoring time.

## Phase 3 — Premium Excel add-in

Entry condition: all add-in gates from Deliverable 6 and Phase 2 gate.

Steps:

1. Windows/Mac Office.js spike for P0 features and shortcut conflicts.
2. Closed beta with 15–25 paid users.
3. Account/two-device licence and self-service deactivation.
4. Telemetry/privacy/security review.
5. AppSource validation or another low-friction production distribution route.
6. Premium bundle test; separate sale only if demand supports it.

Kill if projected incremental 18-month contribution does not cover development, support and opportunity cost.

## Phase 4 — Case-library expansion

Entry condition: second-case efficiency and three-month revenue/quality gate.

Priorities:

- three to five cases with genuinely different decision patterns, not sectors reskinned;
- calibrated difficulty and optional pathways;
- club licences with self-service cohort codes;
- affiliate terms and reconciliation;
- narrow SEO and screen-recorded video engine;
- annual access/update bundle test.

Potential sequence:

1. tech-enabled services buyout;
2. industrial carve-out with working-capital/capex tension;
3. consumer/services case with customer/price-volume ambiguity;
4. private-credit extension only if existing buyers request it.

Do not expand to every private-markets category to inflate TAM.

## Phase 5 — Broader platform opportunities

Entry condition: actual customer behaviour, not strategic imagination.

Candidate adjacencies:

- private credit, if debt/downside modules are most used;
- growth equity, if operating/KPI analysis outperforms LBO content;
- infrastructure, if university/recruiter demand is observed;
- employer/IC training, if firms request licensed cohorts;
- timed-assessment tooling, if recruiters/firms rather than candidates become buyers.

Do not expand into generic investment banking, accounting, broad recruiting or AI scoring without separate evidence.

## Stop/reuse outcome

If Phase 1 fails:

- archive the paid case as a portfolio/research asset;
- optionally publish a stripped, sourced diagnostic as a free article;
- retain the model regression and provenance process;
- do not keep building the viewer/add-in;
- do not reinterpret email signups as product validation;
- review whether founder time is better allocated to a larger-B2B, higher-ticket opportunity.
