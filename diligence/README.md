Status: investment-committee and product/technical diligence completed 16 July 2026. No product implementation is authorised by this pack.

Repository-controlled Phase 0 remediation was subsequently implemented on 16 July 2026. DNS/HTTPS remain deliberately deferred, and professional employment/legal/tax/brand clearance remains open. See [Phase 0 status](../governance/phase-0-status.md).

## Decision

**Validate narrowly. Do not build the proposed platform or Excel add-in yet.**

The repository is a lightly customised Quartz study-note site, not a commercial-product foundation. The business hypothesis is plausible only as a small, evidence-gated niche product. “European” is not a sufficient wedge: a current competitor, Break Into PE, already claims that position, and the large US-led providers have far more content, authority and distribution. The strongest testable wedge is a professionally audited, timed European mid-market investment simulation that tests judgment and communication as well as mechanics.

The recommended first offer is one original case, sold as a complete simulation rather than a library. It should include a free diagnostic slice and a CHF 59 beta / CHF 79 standard full case. The Excel add-in should be represented only by a clickable or video prototype until users demonstrate that it changes purchase behaviour. The browser viewer is useful as experience and access control, not as DRM; it belongs in a minimal commercial product only after preorders.

## Evidence convention

- **Verified fact**: observed in the repository, an official source, or a named current source linked inline.
- **Third-party claim**: a competitor, reviewer or platform claim that has not been independently audited.
- **Estimate**: a range constructed for decision-making; it is not market fact.
- **Founder assumption**: an input supplied in the assignment.
- **Inference**: the diligence conclusion drawn from facts and estimates.
