# Decision record

## IC recommendation

**Recommendation: proceed only to a six-to-eight-week paid validation, with a CHF 3,000 cash cap and a 120-hour founder-time cap. Do not build a case library, account platform, production viewer, or Excel add-in before the gates in the validation plan are met.**

I would invest limited personal time and money in the validation. I would not invest hundreds of hours in the current product plan on the evidence available today.

## Why this is not a straight “proceed”

1. The current repository supplies almost no commercial leverage. It is a public static site with short study notes and no cases, models, users, payments, entitlements or product telemetry.
2. The broad content market is already dense. Peak Frameworks sells a $497 PE course with five three-statement models, seven paper LBOs, 10+ cases, 100+ videos and recruiter distribution. WSO sells a $297 PE course with nine tests and a large interview database. BIWS, Wall Street Prep, Training the Street, Multiple Expansion and YouTube cover the same foundations at prices from free to several thousand dollars.
3. Europe is not white space. Break Into PE explicitly positions itself as Europe-focused and combines digital content with personalised coaching.
4. Static generic cases, explanations and memos are exposed to AI substitution. ChatGPT and Claude each have free tiers and $20/month individual plans; candidates can generate unlimited-looking practice material, even if its quality is unreliable.
5. Distribution is unproven. The founder has no demonstrated audience, partner pipeline, search traffic or paid conversion data in this repository.
6. The add-in has strategically attractive demo value but poor sequencing. Cheap specialists already offer formatting and tracing for roughly $3–6/month, Office add-ins require a hosted web application and cross-platform testing, and some interviewers prohibit add-ins.

## Why it is worth one disciplined test

1. High-stakes candidates pay for confidence, compression and verified realism, not merely information. Established providers demonstrate willingness to pay from roughly $297–$599 for self-study PE products.
2. The founder has unusually relevant subject-matter fit for European mid-market cases, commercial diligence and IC communication.
3. Free resources are strong on mechanics but fragmented on full, timed decision episodes: imperfect CIM, investment thesis, commercial risks, model, memo, presentation and defensible recommendation.
4. A single original case can test demand without platform construction and without using employer materials.
5. University clubs and niche creators can provide falsifiable distribution tests before a public founder brand is required.

## The attack point

Do not attack Peak on quantity, generic recruiting guidance, certificates, recruiter databases, community or creator reach. Do not attack Break Into PE on “Europe.” Attack the gap between learning LBO mechanics and performing an ambiguous investment exercise under time pressure:

- one coherent decision episode, not a course catalogue;
- incomplete and slightly conflicting information;
- explicit commercial and downside reasoning;
- model discipline and presentation quality;
- memo and IC-style oral/written communication;
- observable scoring anchors rather than a model answer alone;
- an audited, internally consistent solution pack;
- a timed browser experience that feels like a real assessment without copying a real firm test.

The product promise should be: **“Rehearse the full decision, not just the LBO.”**

## Kill conditions

Stop after validation if any of these is true:

- fewer than 8 paid orders from at least 150 qualified visitors despite two credible distribution tests;
- fewer than 30% of buyers start the case or fewer than 40% of starters reach the debrief;
- median buyer rating below 8/10 for realism or below 8/10 for usefulness;
- most purchasers say free/AI material was sufficient and cannot name a paid-only outcome;
- fewer than two partners produce tracked qualified traffic after 20 well-targeted approaches;
- founder cannot obtain adequate employment/conflict comfort before public sale;
- creating and independently auditing one case requires more than 120 founder hours on a repeatable basis;
- refund/serious-defect requests exceed 8% in the first cohort;
- the add-in prototype does not increase stated purchase choice by at least 15 percentage points in a randomised offer test.

Passing a gate authorises only the next phase, not the full roadmap.
