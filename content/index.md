## One connected rehearsal, not another video course

The working offer is a self-service investment-committee simulation for candidates who already know basic accounting and LBO mechanics. You receive imperfect fictional company information, build the required outputs and make a decision before seeing an audited solution and observable scoring rubric.

| What you practise                   | What you produce                               |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Commercial prioritisation           | Explicit investment recommendation             |
| LBO and debt mechanics              | Checked transaction and returns outputs        |
| Downside and disconfirming evidence | Conditions and diligence priorities            |
| Decision-led communication          | One-page recommendation and five-slide IC pack |

## Start with a genuine diagnostic

The [[diagnostic/index|free 15-minute diagnostic]] uses a small fictional company snapshot. It tests whether you can connect evidence, one cash-flow calculation and a conditional investment recommendation. It does not require an account and does not pretend to predict hiring outcomes.

## Independent by design

Every company and number used commercially must be independently created or based on appropriately licensed public information. Sources & Uses does not contain employer materials, real dataroom information, leaked interview tests or firm-specific templates. It is not affiliated with any investment firm, recruiter, university or training provider.
