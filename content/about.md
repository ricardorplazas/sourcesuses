# About Sources & Uses

Sources & Uses is an independent prelaunch project testing whether candidates will pay for a realistic, self-service European mid-market private-equity simulation.

The editorial perspective is informed by professional experience in investment analysis, due diligence, financial modelling and European mid-market companies. That experience does not authorise reuse of employer information or materials.

## Independence commitments

- no confidential employer information;
- no copied models, cases, presentations or internal processes;
- no real dataroom information or candidate submissions;
- no leaked interview tests;
- no claim of endorsement by a firm, recruiter, university or training provider;
- fictional or appropriately licensed underlying materials;
- human review and independent checking before commercial release.

Current content is educational, not investment, legal, tax, recruiting or employment advice. See the [[privacy-policy|privacy notice]] and [[terms|validation terms]].
