title: Free PE decision diagnostic
description: A free 15-minute fictional European mid-market PE diagnostic testing commercial prioritisation, debt paydown and investment recommendation.

The diagnostic is deterministic and runs entirely in your browser. It tests six observable decision signals; it does not create a personality type, predict interview performance or transmit your answers automatically.

After completion, you receive the answer path and can export your result as JSON. No marketing consent is bundled into the exercise.
