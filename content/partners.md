# Partner validation kit

Sources & Uses is testing a free 15-minute PE decision diagnostic and one complete fictional case simulation. Partners are asked to share the diagnostic for member feedback—not endorse an unproven paid product.

## Suggested description

> Sources & Uses is an independent prelaunch project testing realistic European mid-market private-equity case practice. Its free 15-minute diagnostic connects commercial evidence, debt paydown and an explicit investment recommendation. It contains no firm-specific or leaked interview materials.

## Suggested tracked link

Append a non-personal partner code supplied by Sources & Uses:

```text
https://ricardorplazas.github.io/sourcesuses/diagnostic/?partner=CLUB-CODE
```

## Disclosure

Any future commission, sponsorship credit or discount must be disclosed at the point of sharing. No university or partner logo may be used without written permission.

## What partners can evaluate

- member relevance and completion;
- usefulness of the deterministic debrief;
- whether the method differs from free explanation content;
- interest in a complete self-service simulation;
- concerns about browser-only case materials or Excel add-ins.

No live workshop is required. A friendly reply does not count as a validation partner; tracked qualified traffic does.
