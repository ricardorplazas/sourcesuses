# How to approach a two-hour PE case study

**Intent:** help a candidate who knows the mechanics turn an unfamiliar company pack into a coherent investment recommendation. **Reviewed:** 16 July 2026.

The scarce resource in a timed case is not Excel functionality. It is attention. A technically elaborate model cannot rescue a recommendation that never identifies what drives business quality, valuation and downside.

## Start with a decision frame

Before reading every page, write five incomplete statements:

1. We should proceed if…
2. The valuation is supportable only if…
3. The most important evidence of business quality is…
4. The downside becomes dangerous when…
5. The next diligence question must resolve…

These are hypotheses, not answers. They tell you what to search for and make it easier to notice disconfirming information.

## Use a four-pass workflow

### Pass 1 — requirements and constraints

Identify the exact deliverables, time limit, permitted assumptions and supplied definitions. Convert them into a submission checklist. Missing a required output is usually more damaging than omitting an optional schedule.

### Pass 2 — commercial decision drivers

Separate information into four buckets:

| Bucket             | Question                                              |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Demand quality     | Why should customers continue buying?                 |
| Operating capacity | What constrains delivery of the plan?                 |
| Economics          | Which volume, price and cost drivers reach cash flow? |
| Evidence risk      | Which management claim is least supported?            |

Do not label revenue “recurring” without explaining the mechanism. A contract, regulatory requirement, switching cost and repeat purchase history are different types of evidence.

### Pass 3 — minimum viable model

Build only what the decision requires: transaction bridge, operating case, cash flow, debt, returns, downside and visible checks. Keep inputs separate from formulas and make every assumption traceable.

### Pass 4 — recommendation and consistency

Make the decision explicit. State price or financing conditions, key evidence, disconfirming evidence and what would change your mind. Reconcile every number in the recommendation and slides to the final model.

## A practical 120-minute allocation

| Activity                             | Minutes |
| ------------------------------------ | ------: |
| Frame requirements and hypotheses    |      10 |
| Review materials and extract drivers |      25 |
| Build and check the model            |      55 |
| Decide and set conditions            |      10 |
| Communicate                          |      15 |
| Final consistency check              |       5 |

Adjust the split to the requested deliverables. The principle is to reserve decision and checking time before modelling expands to fill the exercise.

## Final test

Someone reading only your first page should understand the recommendation, valuation and leverage context, two or three reasons, principal downside and next diligence actions. If they cannot, the output is not yet decision-led.

This is an original editorial method, not a representation of any firm’s interview process.
