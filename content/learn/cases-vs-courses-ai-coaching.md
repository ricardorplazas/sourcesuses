# Cases vs courses, AI tools and coaching

No format is universally best. Choose based on the failure you need to correct.

| Need                                         | Strongest starting format     | Limitation                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Learn accounting or LBO mechanics            | Structured course or textbook | May not rehearse integration under time pressure             |
| Generate unlimited questions or explanations | General AI assistant          | Quality, consistency and model auditing require verification |
| Receive personalised critique                | Coach or experienced reviewer | Expensive and operationally human-dependent                  |
| Observe modelling technique                  | Video walkthrough             | Watching can be mistaken for performing                      |
| Rehearse the complete case independently     | Audited simulation            | Requires the learner to do the work and self-assess honestly |

Sources & Uses is testing the last category. It should be purchased only if a complete timed rehearsal and structured debrief solve a gap that free explanations, existing courses or coaching do not.

The diagnostic is free so you can evaluate the method before expressing purchase interest. This comparison does not claim competitor inferiority or affiliation.
