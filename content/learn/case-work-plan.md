# A 30, 60 and 120-minute PE case work plan

**Intent:** reserve time for the decision and final review. **Reviewed:** 16 July 2026.

| Stage                       | 30 min | 60 min | 120 min |
| --------------------------- | -----: | -----: | ------: |
| Requirements and hypotheses |      4 |      6 |      10 |
| Evidence review             |      9 |     14 |      25 |
| Calculations/model          |      7 |     20 |      55 |
| Decision and conditions     |      4 |      7 |      10 |
| Communication               |      4 |      9 |      15 |
| Final checks                |      2 |      4 |       5 |

For a 30-minute exercise, use only essential calculations and write the recommendation directly. For 60 minutes, add a compact model or structured return bridge. For 120 minutes, build the full decision spine but preserve at least 25 minutes for deciding, communicating and checking.

The work plan should change when the deliverables change. The invariant is to protect time for an explicit recommendation and consistency check.
