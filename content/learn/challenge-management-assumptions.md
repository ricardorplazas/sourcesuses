# How to challenge management assumptions under time pressure

**Intent:** focus challenge on assumptions capable of changing valuation, leverage or recommendation. **Reviewed:** 16 July 2026.

## Rank assumptions by decision sensitivity

Start with price, volume/capacity, retention, gross margin, operating leverage, cash conversion and acquisition contribution. Ignore low-impact detail until these are understood.

## Use an evidence ladder

For each important assumption, ask:

1. What happened historically?
2. What changed to make the future different?
3. Is that change within management’s control?
4. Is leading evidence already visible?
5. What operational capacity is required?
6. What would disconfirm the plan?

## Connect commercial and financial evidence

A 6% pricing assumption is not only a revenue-model cell. Test contract reset dates, renewal behaviour, customer concentration, service criticality and competitor response. A technician-productivity assumption should reconcile to route density, utilisation, hiring, attrition and service quality.

## Translate uncertainty into a decision

Do not finish with “needs more diligence.” State whether uncertainty requires a lower price, different financing, a condition to proceed or a decline.
