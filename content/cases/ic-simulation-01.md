# Rehearse the full investment decision

You are evaluating a fictional DACH compliance-services company for a mid-market sponsor. Headline recurrence and growth look attractive, but pricing evidence, technician capacity, customer concentration and a recent bolt-on complicate the underwriting.

## Exact candidate task

In two hours, you will:

1. review a 24–30 page fictional company and transaction pack;
2. complete transaction, operating, debt, returns and downside outputs;
3. make an explicit proceed, conditional-proceed or decline recommendation;
4. prepare a one-page recommendation and five-slide IC pack;
5. compare your work with an audited solution, an alternative valid conclusion and observable scoring anchors.

## Prerequisites

You should already understand three-statement relationships, enterprise-to-equity bridges, sources and uses, debt schedules, MOIC/IRR and basic Excel modelling. Desktop Excel is expected for the full exercise.
