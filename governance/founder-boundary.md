# Founder operating boundary

Status: founder review and professional confirmation required before commercial sale.

## Purpose

Sources & Uses must remain clearly separate from the founder's employment and must not use confidential, proprietary, or employer-derived material.

## Non-negotiable operating rules

- Work only on personal time, personal equipment, personal accounts, and personally funded services.
- Do not use employer email, storage, software licences, prompts, templates, models, slide masters, processes, case studies, investment memoranda, committee papers, datarooms, candidate work, or contact lists.
- Do not recreate employer materials from memory or make superficial changes to them.
- Do not use information learned under confidentiality obligations, even if names are removed.
- Do not imply employer, fund, recruiter, university, or professional endorsement.
- Keep fictional company identities and data independently created or grounded only in appropriately licensed public information.
- Keep an item-level provenance record for every commercial case asset.
- Escalate uncertainty before using a source; absence of a logo or company name is not sufficient clearance.

## Stop conditions

Do not accept payment or publish commercial case material if:

- approval required by contract/policy has not been obtained;
- the IP ownership of the work is uncertain;
- the project could reasonably be interpreted as competing with or trading on the employer;
- the source or originality of a material asset cannot be demonstrated;
- a safe public credibility strategy cannot be maintained.
