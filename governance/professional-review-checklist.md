# Professional review checklist

Status: open. This checklist identifies questions for qualified Swiss/EU legal, tax, employment, and accounting professionals; it is not advice.

## Employment and IP

- [ ] Review employment contract and incorporated policies.
- [ ] Determine whether side-employment notice or written approval is required.
- [ ] Confirm duty-of-loyalty and competition boundaries.
- [ ] Confirm ownership of work created on personal time/equipment.
- [ ] Confirm restrictions on using professional biography and experience in marketing.
- [ ] Confirm whether anonymous or brand-led marketing changes any obligation.
- [ ] Record written advice/approval reference in the founder boundary memo.

## Swiss operating structure

- [ ] Confirm self-employment/sole-proprietor setup and compensation-office recognition timing.
- [ ] Confirm AHV/social contribution obligations.
- [ ] Confirm income-tax reporting and deductible expense treatment.
- [ ] Confirm bookkeeping and record-retention requirements.
- [ ] Confirm VAT monitoring and registration trigger.
- [ ] Confirm whether a merchant of record changes invoicing/accounting treatment.

## International digital sales

- [ ] Confirm EU/UK and other consumer digital-content VAT treatment.
- [ ] Confirm merchant-of-record contractual allocation and evidence requirements.
- [ ] Confirm consumer withdrawal and immediate digital-supply consent wording.
- [ ] Confirm refund/defect and unfair-terms requirements for target countries.

## Privacy and marketing

- [ ] Identify controller identity and publish a working private contact address.
- [ ] Confirm Swiss FADP and GDPR scope.
- [ ] Review diagnostic/email consent and lawful bases.
- [ ] Review processors, transfers, contracts, and retention schedule.
- [ ] Confirm cookie/analytics consent design before optional tracking.
- [ ] Confirm access, deletion, correction, objection, and complaint procedures.

## Brand and intellectual property

- [ ] Conduct name/trademark clearance for Sources & Uses in relevant classes/markets.
- [ ] Clear every fictional company/product name before release.
- [ ] Review contributor/reviewer IP assignment and confidentiality terms.
- [ ] Review public-repository software/content licence boundary.
- [ ] Review non-affiliation and educational-purpose wording.

## Commercial launch gate

Payment must not be accepted until all launch-critical items are either checked or accompanied by a written professional conclusion explaining why they do not apply.
