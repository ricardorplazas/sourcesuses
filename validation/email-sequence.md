# Consent-aware validation email sequence

These are provider-ready drafts, not an active automation. Send marketing messages only after explicit optional consent; transactional diagnostic delivery must remain separate.

## Immediate — requested result

Subject: Your Sources & Uses diagnostic debrief

Your result is attached or linked as requested. The useful next step is to compare your answer with the six-step decision path, especially where evidence, debt paydown and recommendation diverged.

This message delivers the requested result. It does not add you to marketing unless you separately opted in.

## Day 2 — strong versus weak decision

Subject: A correct model can still produce a weak decision

Contrast a descriptive recommendation with a conditional, evidence-linked decision. End with one link to the recommendation guide.

## Day 5 — full-case preview

Subject: What the two-hour simulation actually requires

Show exact candidate files, debrief outputs, prerequisites, exclusions and current validation status. Do not imply scarcity.

## Day 8 — prioritisation mistake

Subject: The schedule that should not consume your final 20 minutes

Explain the stop rule: check and communicate when the next schedule is less decision-relevant than reconciling the current model.

## Day 12 — offer

Subject: Choose the beta only if this is your gap

Restate price, delivery, technical requirements, refund/withdrawal terms and who should not buy. Suppress purchasers and unsubscribed recipients.

Every marketing email requires a one-click unsubscribe and sender/controller details before activation.
