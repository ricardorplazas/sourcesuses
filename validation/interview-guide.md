# Recent-candidate interview guide

Target 15–20 candidates who attempted a European PE case within 24 months: at least five bankers/transaction professionals, five consultants/adjacent professionals and five students. Cap warm contacts at five.

## Opening

“I am testing a self-service case simulation. I am not selling during this call. Please do not share confidential employer or interview materials.”

## Behaviour before opinion

1. Tell me about the last PE case round you prepared for.
2. What exact materials did you use? Describe the workflow, not confidential case content.
3. What did you spend and what triggered the purchase?
4. Where did preparation fail to resemble the actual task?
5. Which deliverable—model, recommendation or presentation—created the most uncertainty?
6. What did free videos or AI tools solve well? What remained unreliable or incomplete?
7. Were Excel add-ins permitted or installable on the machine you used?

## Forced choice

Show identical preview materials, then ask the participant to choose:

- one audited integrated simulation at CHF 59;
- one audited integrated simulation at CHF 79;
- a generic case bundle at the same price;
- an hour of personalised review at a stated higher price;
- none.

Ask what evidence is missing before they would complete checkout. Do not ask “Would you buy?” without a price and alternative.

## Prototype tasks

1. Find the customer-concentration page in the case-room prototype.
2. Start and pause the timer.
3. Explain whether browser-only pages hinder the intended workflow.
4. Use the five-command add-in concept and choose between an add-in bundle and a second-case bundle at the same price.

## Closing evidence

- What would make the product clearly worth paying for instead of generating a case with AI?
- What would make you request a refund?
- May we record coded, non-identifying findings and contact you for beta testing?

Record facts and direct behavioural evidence separately from interpretation. Do not store names in the repository.
